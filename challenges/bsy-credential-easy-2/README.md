# Ghost Paste Relay

A hacker collective's paste relay has drifted back onto the university range, still broadcasting its old neon sign like a signal from a forgotten net. The relay holds a record the crew meant to keep private.

Find the service on the lab network, gain access, and recover the protected record. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`.
