# Actuator Night Shift

A robotics lab's actuator console has stopped answering during a late shift, and the maintenance crew needs the protected record recovered before the next test run.

The challenge runs on the lab network. Scan **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141-199**. Find the reachable lab services, gain access to the protected console record, and recover its flag.
