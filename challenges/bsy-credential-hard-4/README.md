# Last Airlock: Ghost Credentials

Kestrel-9 has been drifting cold since the last crew rotation, but something on the station still answers. The course team believes a protected airlock record was left behind.

Recover that record from the services you can reach on the lab network. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`.

The station has gone quiet. The airlock has not.
