# Actuator After Hours

A robotics lab’s actuator console is still answering on the course network after the night shift. The maintenance crew says a recovery credential was left somewhere reachable, while the console keeps its recovery record behind an account.

Scan the lab network at `172.20.0.0/24` (challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`). Find the console, obtain access, and recover the protected record.
