# Mars Relay: Unknown Signal

A rover on the red planet stopped transmitting after its telemetry relay began answering ground control with an unfamiliar signal. The mission team needs the archived telemetry record to reconstruct the last transmission.

The relay and a few ground-station lookalikes are running on the lab network. Scan **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141-199**. Identify the relay from how its services respond, then recover the archived record.

The range is isolated to the lab network. Happy hunting, operator.
