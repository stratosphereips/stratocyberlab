# Dead Air on the Black Channel

A pirate-radio crew has lost the transmission record from its remote broadcast rig. The rig is still on the air somewhere in the course range, and your job is to recover that record.

This challenge runs on the lab network. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Read what the rig sends back and recover the broadcast record.
