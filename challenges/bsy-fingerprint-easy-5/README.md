# Last Call at the High-Score Cabinet

The Pixel Palace arcade cabinet disappeared during a blackout, and its final high score never made it onto the public leaderboard. Its archived record is still somewhere on the lab network. Find the cabinet and recover that record.

The range is local to the lab. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Listen closely to what the network services say: the cabinet has a few decoy signals mixed into its after-hours noise.
