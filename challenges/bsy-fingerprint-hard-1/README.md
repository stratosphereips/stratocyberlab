# Dead Air at the Neon BBS

A retro BBS gateway has gone quiet after its night-shift operator vanished. The campus archive needs the lost transmission recovered before the next solar flare scrambles the relay logs.

Your goal is to recover the transmission record from the gateway. The challenge runs on the lab network: scan **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141–172.20.0.199**. Several listeners are active, and their ports may not tell the whole story.
