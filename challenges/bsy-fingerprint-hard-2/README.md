# Dead Letter from the Mainframe

A vintage mainframe terminal has reappeared on the university range. Its surrounding legacy systems are chattering, but one may still hold an important audit record.

Your goal is to recover that record from the service that owns it. The challenge runs only on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`, where challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`.

The array is noisy, the hardware is old, and the port labels may not tell the whole story. Good hunting, operator.
