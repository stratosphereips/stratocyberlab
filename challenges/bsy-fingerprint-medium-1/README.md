# Dead Air on the Ghost Frequency

The pirate-radio crew of the *Nightjar* has vanished from the airwaves. Their relay rig was last heard carrying a short emergency broadcast, and the archive team needs that record recovered before the signal fades.

Find the rig on the lab network and recover the broadcast record. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. The rig is reachable only from inside the lab network.
