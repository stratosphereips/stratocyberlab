# The Midnight Mainframe

A retired university mainframe has flickered back to life, and its old terminals are answering across the lab network. Your task is to identify the system holding an archived record and recover its contents.

This challenge runs on the lab network. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Several ports may answer, so observe what each service says when you connect.
