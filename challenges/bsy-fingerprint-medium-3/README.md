# Red Dust Relay

A Mars rover's telemetry relay went quiet after a dust storm cut contact with mission control. The recovery team believes the black-box record is still somewhere on the relay.

Recover the missing record. The challenge runs on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. The relay may not behave the way its port numbers suggest.
