# Ghost Signals of the Orbital Relay

A derelict orbital relay station has dropped off the university’s telemetry grid. Its remaining systems still answer from the dark, and the port labels offer little assurance about what is listening.

Your goal is to recover the station’s archived black-box record. The challenge runs entirely on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Enumerate open ports, connect to the services, and use their responses to determine which one can provide the record.
