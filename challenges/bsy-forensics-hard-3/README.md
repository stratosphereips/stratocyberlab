# Ghost in the Mainframe

A cracked retro mainframe gateway has come back online after a suspicious maintenance window. The university's incident team needs you to reconstruct what happened on the host and recover the intruder's proof.

The box runs on the lab network. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Use the provided login credentials to investigate the host's accounts, records, and filesystem traces.

Bring your terminal, trust the timestamps only after checking them, and don't assume the loudest signal is the answer.
