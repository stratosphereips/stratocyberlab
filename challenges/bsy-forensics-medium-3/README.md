# Lunar Node: Quiet Hours

A research cluster node on the lunar base network missed its routine check-in after an unattended shift. The operations team suspects someone used the quiet hours to leave evidence behind.

Log into the host, reconstruct relevant activity from its ordinary Linux forensic surfaces, and recover the concealed evidence. This challenge runs on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`.

Use the access details supplied with the challenge. Identify evidence connected to the incident and submit what it contains. Routine maintenance and unrelated activity are part of the scene.
