# Honeytoken Deception

Asterion, a bank of the future, has an API exposed on the lab network. Its access infrastructure has attracted attention after a suspicious event, and the treasury team needs a clean recovery of its reserve archive.

Your goal is to recover the flag from the API. The service is on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Enumerate the service and compare the evidence it exposes.
