# Honeytoken Deception

A megacorp's credential vault was pulled from a derelict relay, and its records are mixed with planted decoys. Your job is to recover the protected proof without trusting every asset you find.

The challenge runs on the lab network. Scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`. Enumerate the exposed service, compare its evidence, and recover the vault's proof using the genuine asset.
