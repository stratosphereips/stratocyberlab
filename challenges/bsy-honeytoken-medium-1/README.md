# Ghost Keys of Kepler Station

An abandoned orbital station is still answering on the StratoCyberLab network. Its last operations log may be the only clue to what happened to the crew.

Search the lab network at **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141-199**. Recover the station record left for an authorized operator.

The station is quiet, but its systems still remember who tried to get in.
