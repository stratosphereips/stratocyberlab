# Ghosts on the BBS

Relay-9 is a retro bulletin-board system that has started attracting curious callers. Its operators left behind a public-facing record set, and the last recovery message is still somewhere on the board.

Your goal is to recover that message. The challenge runs on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24` (challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`) and investigate the system's available records.

Good hunting, sysop. Keep your modem handshakes clean.
