# Ghostlight Vault

A megacorp’s credential vault has gone dark after an overnight incident. The audit team recovered access records, but some may be bait. Identify the genuine asset and recover the protected vault record.

The challenge runs on the lab network. Scan **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141–172.20.0.199**. Compare the evidence you find before acting: the vault is monitored, and false leads give convincing responses.
