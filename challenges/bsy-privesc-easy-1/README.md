# Mainframe After Hours

A cracked gateway still hosts a retro time-sharing account, and a protected record is missing from the incident archive. Log in to the box, inspect the host, and recover that record by gaining the access needed to read it. The challenge runs on the lab network: scan `172.20.0.0/24`; challenge hosts are in `172.20.0.141-199`.

SSH access is provided in the challenge details. Your goal is to recover the flag from the host.
