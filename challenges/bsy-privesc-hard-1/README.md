# Ghost in the Maintenance Deck

A starship's maintenance console keeps transmitting from a quiet corner of the training range. The vendor crew left the ship in the hands of a junior deckhand, and the sealed maintenance record is still aboard.

Log in with the supplied crew credentials, inspect the host, and recover the record. This service runs on the lab network. Scan **172.20.0.0/24**; challenge hosts are in **172.20.0.141-199**.
